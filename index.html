JazzTimeline

An open, cited dataset of jazz recordings, personnel and apprenticeship lineage, and an instrument for studying them.

What this is

Most jazz history is told in chapters. This is built to be queried instead: who played with whom, where a session was cut, on whose label, who taught whom, and what the surviving documents actually support.

Every claim carries its source, and every source carries a tier. Nothing is inferred, and absence from the record is reported as absence from the record, never as fact.

Principles

  • The data is the deliverable. The interface is replaceable.
  • Nothing here needs sound to be read.
  • Factual data is public domain; editorial work is attributed.
  • Queries are citable, and every superlative states its denominator.
The dataset and the code are being built in the open at github.com/jazztimeline.
