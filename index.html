JazzTimeline

A new way to configure questions about the music and the people who made it.

Jazz history reaches us through biographies, liner notes, oral histories, documentaries and discographies. This approach sits alongside those: the same history, open from any perspective, the emphasis depending only on one’s curiosity. Who shared a bandstand. Where a session was cut, and for which label. Who came up under whom. And the details of the lives behind the music — the whole cast of characters in the story of jazz, not the players alone.

Being built now, methodically.
